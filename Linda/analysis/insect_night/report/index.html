Observed insect activity at night

Night-only preparation of the Global Dataset of Diel Activity Patterns in Insect Communities. This stage establishes geographic coverage and measurement integrity. It does not claim an artificial-light effect or nocturnal preference.

Ready for geographic plotting. All 593 night records have valid coordinates. Quantitative particle sizing remains withheld because raw magnitudes are not comparable across studies, units, taxa, and sampling protocols.

Dataset grain

A source row is one reported measurement for a taxonomic group, sampling context, measured property, measurement level, and diel period. The night-only table preserves all source rows. The canonical table collapses paired mean/total representations by preferring the reported mean.
