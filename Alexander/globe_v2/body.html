Method
The same image and ZT coordinate are shared by both conditions. Activity particle count, speed, and trail strength follow the current minute-level cage mean. Gene expression and melatonin are measured only at four ZT markers; values between those markers use circular linear interpolation. Ambient breathing makes the encoding visible while paused; its frequency is not a biological measurement. Every gene keeps its own spoke and must be compared with that same gene across condition, tissue, or time. Brain–liver difference is descriptive only: it is the absolute difference between the two current tissue values, not a correlation coefficient and not physical flow.
