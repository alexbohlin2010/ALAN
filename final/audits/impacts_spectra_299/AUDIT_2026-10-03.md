## Method

- **Independence.** I wrote and ran b00 to b02 (and the checks that followed) **before** opening `06_spectra_299.py`, and nothing imports from `pipeline/`. Route: `pdftocairo -ps` (not SVG as the pipeline does), own parser of the PostScript operators (`rg`, `m`, `l`, `c`, `h`, `re`, `f`), own Bézier flattening (16 chords), own tick OLS, own crossing code. I only looked at the JSON's field layout (not the script) before writing b02.
- Scripts run from `final/` with system `python3` (stdlib only) and poppler, in this order: b01, b02, b07 start, b03, b04, b05, b06, b08, b07 end. `results/b09_hashes_audited_files.txt` holds the SHA-256 of the audited JSON (`e589915c...c9513`) and script (`16f90a8a...ebbf`).
- Visual check: figure rendered with `pdftoppm` (400 dpi and 600 dpi) into the session scratchpad (not in `final/`); b04 draws the JSON samples (one dot per nm) over the 600 dpi render using my own calibration. All six curves sit on the drawn lines/edges: blue apex at 454 nm about 0.92, amber 0.90, red 1.00, white sensitivity 0.99 at 575 nm, white shoulder about 0.75 at 415 nm, the two low white-LED curves (cool white with the small 453 nm bump at 0.16, warm white broad hump at 0.15 near 618 nm).

## Pipeline script vs its stated rules

| Rule in the docstring | Implemented |
|---|---|
| SVG via pdftocairo, page coordinates, Béziers flattened to 8 chords | yes (paths with a `transform` or more than one moveto are skipped) |
| Ticks = grey 20 percent small rectangles; left of the panel = y, below = x; labels taken in order; OLS per axis, largest residual written | yes (asserts 4 and 5 ticks) |
| LED curves = filled outlines with more than 1000 vertices spanning more than half the panel; value = midpoint of highest and lowest crossing; every whole nm inside the x range; median stroke height written | yes (asserts exactly 5; skips columns with fewer than 2 crossings) |
| LED names by least-squares blend fit of line colour to legend swatch, 1:1 | yes (assert that the names are a permutation) |
| P. scintillans = the one white polygon with more than 100 vertices; highest crossing (top edge) at every whole nm | yes (asserts exactly 1) |
| Output rounding: values 1e-4, wavelength exact | yes |

The script writes only `data/derived/impacts/spectra_299.json` and reads the PDF read-only. The extra JSON fields (`line_colour`, `legend_blend_alpha`, peaks, documented block) are derived from the same data or typed constants and are all mentioned or implied in the docstring. I found nothing it does that the docstring does not say.

## Notes for the human (not failures)

- **N1.** Sharp apexes read slightly low with the midpoint rule: estimated true apex (outline top minus half stroke) vs the extracted peak: red 1.0000 vs 0.9982, amber 0.9038 vs 0.9013, blue 0.9319 vs 0.9246 (the blue difference also includes whole-nm sampling: the apex is at about 454.5 nm). That is 0.002 to 0.007 of full scale at the peak tips only; elsewhere the midpoint is the line centre. `peak_value_extracted` for red is 0.9982 although the plot reaches 1.00.
- **N2.** Warm white (618, +1.6 to +1.8 nm) and blue (454, about -0.9 to -1.3 nm) differ from the paper's documented peaks by more than 0.5 nm. Warm white is a flat hump (within 0.001 of the max over several nm), so its peak position is imprecise; for blue, the drawn curve itself peaks near 454.5 nm. These are differences between the plotted figure and the paper text, not extraction errors (my route gives the same wavelengths).
- **N3.** Ranges differ by one sample: red, warm and cool white run 400 to 775 nm, amber and blue 400 to 774 nm, because those two outlines end at 774.84 nm (stroke end). P. scintillans spans exactly 350 to 675 nm.
- **N4.** P. scintillans has 4 samples written `-0.0` (rounded tiny negatives at the baseline; valid JSON, equal to zero).
- **N5.** Tick values (400..700, 0.00..1.00) and the legend names are outlined glyphs, so neither the pipeline nor my extraction parses them; both take them as printed and I verified them by eye on the render (plus the swatch colour fits and the documented peaks).
- **N6.** In `documented`, `flash_peak_nm` 567.47 is the paper's value for P. obscurellus (Hall et al. 2016), as the paper text shows; the key does not name the species. The sensitivity curve is P. scintillans (Lall et al. 1988), as the caption states.
- **N7.** The y axis is "Relative intensity" with the photon/energy basis not stated; the JSON says so. The P. scintillans curve's maximum is 0.9977 (not exactly 1.0), as drawn.
- Raster/PS detail for anyone re-running b03: cairo's PS output uses a page height of 783 pt (true 782.362), a 0.638 pt vertical offset between the PS frame and the raster frame; calibration is unaffected because ticks and curves share the PS frame. I fixed the sign empirically (the other sign doubled the offset), see the comment in b03.
