## Facts noticed (no rule broken; for the humans)
1. **No-data flags at coarser levels.** Under the stated rule, a block is flagged only when *all* of its in-raster pixels are −1.5. The scattered 2025 −1.5 pixels therefore mostly disappear from the flags above native: flag-2 pixels are 1,977 at level 9, 3 at level 8, and 0 at levels 0–7. The 2015 last-row −1.5 pixels stay flagged at every level from 3 to 9 (413 to 35,995 px), because those blocks hold only that one raster row. Mixed blocks show the mean of the remaining pixels.
2. **Spot-check stratum C.** All 5 drawn −1.5 samples are on the raster's last row (65.0°S), since 35,728 of the 37,970 pixels that are −1.5 in either year are there (35,995 + 1,977, minus 2 pixels that are −1.5 in both years). The scattered −1.5 pixels are not represented. The humans can choose to add a stratum for them.
3. **Raw pixel size.** The raw files store 0.0041666667° (rounded 1/240), so the last row's centre computes to −65.000001°. The value is carried unchanged into the COGs and `tiles.json`. Over the full width the difference from 1/240 is about 3e−6° (~0.3 m).
4. During the audit, the agent fixed a bug in its own `a03_curve.py`: it crashed while printing numpy booleans after writing the results. The agent then reran it in full. The results above come from that rerun.
