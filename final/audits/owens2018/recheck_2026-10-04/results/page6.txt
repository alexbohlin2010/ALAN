Discussion
Our results suggest that A. fictais not visually responsive to red wavelengths. However, short
wavelength ambient light (including blue) significantly affects their signaling behavior, induc-
ing increased flash intensity as well as decreased flash frequency. Erratic measurements
obtained during bright (10×) exposures across wavelengths may reflect the small sample size,
necessitated by the limited population numbers and highly seasonal life history of A. ficta, or
