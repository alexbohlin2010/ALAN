## Findings

**C1. `units.sd` expands "SD" to "standard deviation". Severity: info. No value is wrong.**
The paper only prints "SD" (no occurrence of the words "standard deviation" in the 14 pages, checked by grep on the page text). The expansion is the universal reading and the entry says "as printed", so this is cosmetic. If strictness is wanted, the entry could just say 'SD, as printed ("x̄ ± SD" in the Table 1 header)'.

**C2. Other qualitative Results/Discussion sentences are not captured. Severity: info. No value is wrong.**
These were not in F2 and are not required by it: p. 5 "Increases in flash intensity ... were accompanied by significant reductions in flash frequency" and "More extreme reductions in flash frequency were seen when males were exposed to bright intensities"; p. 6 "Due to the consequent lack of data, average peak intensity ... became erratic", "individuals often appeared to remain at least partially affected by the previous series of exposures (Fig 1A and 1B)", and the sentence "Thus, the brightness of male flashes was apparently only influenced by the current level of ambient illumination, while the total number of flashes per minute was determined by both current and previous light exposures". The file states its scope as the quantitative reported results plus selected quoted statements, and does not claim to be exhaustive.

**C3. `not_reported[0]` says the paper does not give "the individual values" while Fig 1 draws two individual males' recordings. Severity: info.** Fig 1 has its own `not_reported` entry (drawn only, not digitised), so the two entries together are accurate. No numeric per-male values are printed anywhere in the PDF text.

**C4. `text_p_values[*].page` is now int (19 values) or the string "5-6" (1 value). Severity: info.** Statements use int pages. A consumer that expects one type needs to handle the single span value (bright frequency 533 nm, which really does span the break).

None of C1 to C4 needs action for correctness. The earlier first-audit remark that the Fig 2 caption "N = 6" is on p. 4 is not what this re-check found: the "(N = 6)" is on p. 7; `units.sd` does not give a page, so the JSON is unaffected.
