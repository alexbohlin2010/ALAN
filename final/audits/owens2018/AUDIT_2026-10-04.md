## Findings

**F1. Three text-vs-Table 1 disagreements are in the paper itself (correctly recorded). Severity: info. No value in the JSON is wrong.**
Verified on the rendered pages 5 and 6:
- dim, flash intensity, 488 nm: Results text "p = 0.017", Table 1 "0.018"
- dim, flash frequency, 463 nm: text "p = 0.049", Table 1 "0.0049"
- bright, flash frequency, 463 nm: text "p <0.0001", Table 1 "0.00042"
(In the Results text, dim intensity 463 nm "p = 0.01" and Table "0.010" are the same number and are counted as agreeing.) The JSON lists exactly these three and resolves none.

**F2. Qualitative results from the Results/Abstract not captured and not in `not_reported`. Severity: low. No value is wrong.**
Present in the PDF, absent from both `statements` and `not_reported`:
- p. 5: "The decline is due to increases in interpulse interval [58] and, to a lesser extent, flash duration (S2 Table)"
- p. 5: "sporadic periods without flashing were more common under illumination, sometimes continuing for 10 or more seconds"
- p. 5 (Methods end): "Exposure order explained a large amount of observed variation in flash frequency ... did not have an observable impact on flash intensity" (the ANOVA numbers on p. 6 are captured)
- p. 1 (Abstract): "When exposed to wavelengths at or below 533 nm, males emitted brighter signals with decreased frequency"
- p. 5 Fig 1 caption units ("average counts per 100 ms over 4 min") are used in `units`, but Fig 1 itself is not mentioned in `not_reported` (Fig 2 is).
The file does not claim to be exhaustive for the Results text, and it states its scope as the quantitative reported results.

**F3. Two `units` entries go slightly beyond the paper's wording. Severity: low. No value is wrong.**
- `units.sd` says SD is "over the six analysed males". The paper prints "x̄ ± SD" in Table 1 and gives N = 6 (Fig 2 caption, p. 4); it does not say in so many words that the Table 1 SD is across the six males. (Frequency means such as 43.167 = 259/6 are consistent with n = 6, but that is the auditor's observation, not in the paper.)
- `units.p` says "GLMM, wavelength as fixed effect vs the initial dark recordings; dim and bright analysed separately". This is from the Methods (p. 4); the Table 1 / Results text does not itself say the p-values are the GLMM outputs.
Both sit under `units`, which has no label of its own.

**F4. `page` of all 20 `text_p_values` is the string "5-6", statements use integers. Severity: info.**
Actual locations: dim lists and bright frequency 444-515 nm on p. 5; bright frequency 533 nm straddles the 5/6 break; bright intensity list on p. 6. "5-6" is true but coarser than the data allows, and the type differs from `statements.*.page` (int).

**F5. Author conclusions are included as quoted statements. Severity: info.**
`red_not_responsive` (p. 6), `recommendation` (p. 9) and `intensity_increase` (p. 5, contains "significantly increased") are the authors' interpretive sentences, quoted verbatim and labelled documented with page. The key names are chosen by the script. No interpretive text written by the agent was found in the JSON.

**F6. Table 1 bold p-values: observation. Severity: info.**
In the render, bold p-values are exactly those below 0.05 in both blocks (dim intensity 0.0021, 0.010, 0.018, 0.021; frequency 0.0042, 0.0049, 0.000014, 0.0098, 0.046; bright frequency 0.0019, 0.00042, 0.000014, 0.0018, 0.032). The paper does not define bold; the JSON records nothing about it and lists this in `not_reported`, which is accurate.

**F7. Number formats and file metadata. Severity: info.**
- `1.4e-05` (two Table 1 cells and one text-vs-table entry) is exponent notation for printed "0.000014"; value equal, `printed` keeps the original. Trailing zeros are not kept in numeric fields (0.010 -> 0.01, 0.30 -> 0.3, 54.00 -> 54.0, 14.80 -> 14.8); they are kept in `printed`.
- Raw PDF mode is `-rw-r--r--` (owner-writable); MANIFEST says "made read-only". The same mode applies to the other files in that folder. The auditor changed nothing; sha256 unchanged at start and end.
- `owens2018_pdftext.txt` is matched by `.gitignore` (`data/derived/impacts/*`, no exception); the script docstring says it is not committed, and this agrees.

## Anything concerning

Nothing that affects a value. Items needing a human look are only F2 (qualitative results not listed anywhere) and F3 (two `units` phrases not in the paper's words).
