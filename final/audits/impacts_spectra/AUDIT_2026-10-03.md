## Method

- **Independence.** a01 uses `pdftocairo -svg` plus `pdftotext -bbox`, parses `<path>` elements and the transforms with its own regexes, and calibrates with my own OLS from tick marks to the printed labels (400..800 and 0.0..0.5, paired by order and then verified against label centres). It imports nothing from `pipeline/`. I wrote and ran a01 and a02 **before** opening `05_spectra_278.py`.
- Scripts run from `final/` with system `python3` (stdlib only), in this order: a03 start, a01, a02, a05, a04 (seed 20261003), a03 end. a06 holds the SHA-256 of the audited JSON and script (`c7091c1e...ba081` and `ea275eff...08d3`).
- Visual check: page 1 rendered with `pdftoppm` into the session scratchpad (not in `final/`): blue peak about 0.55 at about 478 nm, magenta shoulder about 0.34 at about 608 nm, maximum about 0.38 near 640 nm, magenta end about 0.04 at 780 nm, all as extracted.

## Pipeline script vs its stated rules

| Rule in the docstring | Implemented |
|---|---|
| SVG via pdftocairo, X = a*x, Y = d*y + f | yes (asserts b = c = e = 0) |
| Curves = the two coloured strokes with more than 10 vertices; swatches (2 vertices) identify short/long by nearest legend word | yes (asserts exactly 2 colours) |
| Ticks = black 2-vertex strokes < 5 pt, paired to the nearest numeric label (error if > 8 pt) | yes |
| Per-axis least-squares fit, largest residual stored | yes |
| One sample per vertex, no resampling/smoothing/clipping; only M and L commands | yes (stops otherwise) |
| Output rounded | 0.01 nm and 1e-5 (not stated in the docstring; see N2) |

The script writes only `data/derived/impacts/spectra_278.json`; it reads the PDF read-only.

## Notes for the human (not failures)

- **N1.** Unit string: the printed axis says "mW/m2" but the integral check shows the quantity behaves as a spectral density (mW/m2 per nm). The JSON keeps the printed wording.
- **N2.** Output rounding (2 and 5 decimals) is agent-chosen and not in the docstring; it is below the resolution of the figure.
- **N3.** `peak_nm_extracted` is the first global maximum (long: 641.0) and sits next to `documented.peak_nm` (608), so the two fields disagree for the long curve. They do not contradict (608 is a local maximum, 641 the global one), but a reader of the JSON alone could be confused. Short has a two-vertex flat top (478.06 and 479.05); the first is reported.
- **N4.** The 5 slightly negative long-curve values (above) are calibration noise; any consumer that needs non-negative spectra will have to decide how to treat them.
- **N5.** Samples are the vertices of the drawn path (uneven spacing, 1-9 nm) and the line has a 2 pt stroke width; the extracted value is the centreline.
- The long-lambda legend text says M/P 0.11 and the short says 1.45, while the caption says 1.49 (already noted in MANIFEST). Not part of the data audited.
