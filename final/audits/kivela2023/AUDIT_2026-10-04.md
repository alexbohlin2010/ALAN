| ID | Severity | Wrong value? | Finding |

| F1 | Low | No | Table 1's `Filter` column is not in `paper.table1` and not in `not_reported`: Color-foil 165 "Daylight blue" (blue), Diffusion filter 129 "Heavy frost" (white), Color-foil 104 "Deep amber" (yellow), Color-foil 164 "Flame red" (red). | F2 | Low | No | Results text partly captured: `count_result` holds only the first Results sentence. Not stored and not listed in `not_reported`: the sentences that yellow and red did not differ significantly from control and white did not differ from blue (Results para. 1, PDF p. 4); the whole second Results paragraph apart from 56.9 % (blue and white lower than yellow, red, control; no difference among yellow/red/control or blue/white; "results from the two analyses were qualitatively the same"). The Table 2–4 p-values contain the same information. Also not stored: Methods details outside the 15 statements (1 km road, sites ≥ 40 m apart, light source models and output settings, resistors 2000 Ω, spectrophotometer at 20 cm in a dark room); the Table 4 sub-header line "Pairwise comparisons of numbers of attracted males" (as printed, though the model is presence/absence). `not_reported` lists only 4 items. Selection of statements is a design choice; the gap is only that the omissions are not listed. |
| F3 | Low | No | Docstring says checks per treatment include "n traps, sum of males". The code has no per-treatment n-traps or per-treatment sum-of-males check (only totals: 299 traps, 624 males, 170 successes). Per-treatment n is implied by 0 + ≥1 counts equalling Table 3, and I verified 60/60/60/59/60 and the per-treatment male sums (control 209, blue 60, white 29, yellow 151, red 175 → 624) myself. |
| F4 | Low | No | Checks not covering things the paper states: control lux and photons (0, 0) vs Table 1 control row (no check; values do agree: lux 0, photons 0); `fig2_n` (N = 60 / 59) and the 12 June–4 July dates are not checked; 3 replicates per night and 4 per site per treatment are not checked (I verified them: only night 10 / site 3 yellow deviate). Median check targets are hard-coded from the prose (0 for blue/white, else 2), which is correct. |
| F5 | Info | No | Red photons check passes only on the inclusive boundary (|12.65e12 − 12.70e12| = 5e10 = half unit). Correct under half-up rounding; a half-even reader would expect 1.26e13. The check therefore confirms consistency, not which rounding the paper used. |
| F6 | Info | No | RULES 2.3 labels are at file or statement level, not per table row or per trap row. `traps` lux and photons are labelled `measured` ("treatment light values per trap"), but in the sheet each treatment has exactly one value (lux 8/5/5.6/0/6; photons 1.265e13/6.448e12/5.272e12/0/4.214e12) repeated on every row, and the paper (p. 4) says these were measured once per treatment in a dark room at 20 cm above ground with a cosine corrector, not at the traps. That measurement context is in the paper but not next to the data in the JSON. |
