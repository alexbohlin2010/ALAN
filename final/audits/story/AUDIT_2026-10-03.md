## Method

- **Independence.** The scripts in `audits/story/` import nothing from `pipeline/`, including `pipeline/radiance/config.py`. The constants are restated from the human decisions in `common.py`. I wrote the recomputation from those definitions *before* reading `pipeline/story/*.py`, then read the pipeline scripts only for the definition check.
- **Global (A)** is computed from the raw gzip files through `/vsigzip/`, not from the COGs. GDAL's auxiliary-file writing (PAM) is off, so nothing is written next to the raw files.
- **Countries (B)** use the COGs, which an earlier audit found bit-identical to the raw files (`audits/radiance/AUDIT_2026-10-03.md`, a02; the COG mtimes predate that audit). The 44 spot-check values were read from raw and are equal in the COGs.
- **Masks.** Method A is my own scanline point-in-polygon in numpy: even-odd rule, pixel centre, edges straight in lon/lat. Method B is a separate GDAL `RasterizeLayer` call (ALL_TOUCHED=FALSE). Method C is the exact area of each polygon on the same sphere, using ∬R² cos φ dφ dλ with edges straight in lon/lat.
- Run from `final/` with `.venv/bin/python audits/story/sNN_*.py`. Order: s01 start, s02, s03, s04, s06, s05 `<seed>`, s01 end.

## 2. Geometry (pixel area and geotransform)

- **Geotransform as stored:** origin (−180.00208333335, 75.00208333335), pixel 0.0041666667° (a rounded 1/240). The edge latitudes run from 75.0020833° to −65.0020845°.
  - Row centres run from 75.0° to **−65.00000112°**, the last row.
  - Column centres run from −180.0° to **+180.0000029°**, the duplicate column.
  - Both scripts use the north edge `y0 + r·dy` and the south edge `y0 + (r+1)·dy` for each row, which is correct for dy < 0. They do not shift by half a pixel.
- **Pixel-area sum:** the sum over all pixels (477,492,852 km²) equals R²·(W·Δλ)·(sin top − sin bottom) to the last bit. It is larger than the analytic 75°N–65°S band (2πR²(sin 75° + sin 65°) = 477,481,001 km²) by **11,851 km² (+0.0025%)**. That difference is fully accounted for (residual < 1e-7 km²):
  - 6,321 km² from the half-pixel overhang at the top and bottom edges;
  - 5,530 km² from the 86,401 columns and the stored pixel size, of which 5,526 km² is the one duplicate column.
- **Valid area** after pairwise exclusion: 477,489,298.59 km² in both the pipeline and the audit (relative difference 2.5e-15).

- **Window and offset maths.** Each pipeline window contains the whole mask; my tight bounding boxes lie inside it, 0–2 pixels in from its edges. Window reads and mask placement agree exactly: 0 differing pixels and identical sums.
- **Crimea.** In `admin_0_countries`, Crimea lies outside Ukraine. Natural Earth's Crimea polygon (disputed B89, "Admin. by Russia; Claimed by Ukraine") covers 179,076 pixels, and **0** of them are in the Ukraine mask. Simferopol, Sevastopol and Kerch are not in Ukraine (point checks).
- **Donetsk/Luhansk.** Polygons C02 and C03 ("Self admin.; Claimed by Ukraine") are **100%** inside the Ukraine mask (65,382 and 58,000 pixels).
- **Corsica.** FXX has 10 parts. The Corsica box (8.4–9.7°E, 41.3–43.1°N) drops 2 of them: the main island (8,652 km²) and a 0.75 km² islet at 9.46–9.48°E, 42.62–42.65°N, near Corsica's east coast. The Îles d'Hyères (6.2°E) and the Atlantic islands are kept. The area dropped from the mask is 8,651 km², against 8,653 km² for the two polygons.
- **No overseas France.** FXX extends over −5.13…9.56°E and 41.37…51.09°N. French Guiana, Réunion, Martinique, Guadeloupe and Mayotte are separate map units. The countries-layer 'France' (635,737 km², overseas included) was not used.
- **India (fact, default line).** Every India-administered disputed polygon is 100% in the mask: B00–B05, B88 and C01. Every polygon administered by Pakistan or China (B06–B09), and the **Siachen Glacier** (B45, a separate "Indeterminate" unit in Natural Earth), is 0% in the mask.
- **Pairwise exclusion inside countries:** India has 8 pixels (all −1.5 in 2025); France and Ukraine have none.
