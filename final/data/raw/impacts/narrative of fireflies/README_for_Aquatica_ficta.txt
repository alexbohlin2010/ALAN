The files labeled by date (e.g. "May13.txt") contain spectrophotometer readings from the male tested on that date (average signal intensity between 564.42 and 569.10 nm, over 32 minutes). The corresponding keys (e.g. "May13.key.txt") can be used to interpret these readings. The left column is the randomized sequence of wavelength exposures on that evening, and the order of intensity (light first or dark first, also randomized) is indicated by the values given from left to right; the larger of the two values corresponds to the more intense (10x) exposure. 1 min exposures are always preceded by 1 min of darkness; this "dark exposure" is indicated with a D (e.g. "D2.7"), and the light exposure with an L (e.g. "L2.7"). There are 32 exposures listed in each key; this sequence -- read from left to right, top to bottom -- should be matched to the 32 minute spectrophotometer recording.
